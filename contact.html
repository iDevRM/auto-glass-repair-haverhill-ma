What Happens When You Call?

1

We ask four questions

Year, make, model, and whether there is a camera behind your mirror. That last one is what separates a $300 job from a $900 one, so we ask it first rather than discovering it on your driveway.

2

You get a real number, on that call

Not a starting‑from figure. If recalibration is needed it is in the quote. If we think your damage is repairable rather than a replacement, we will say so — even though it bills less.

3

We book a time and place that suits you

Your driveway, your street, or your workplace car park. We need access to the vehicle and, in winter, somewhere sheltered enough for the adhesive to cure properly.

4

No pressure if you want to think

Plenty of people call to find out whether a chip can wait. Sometimes the honest answer is yes. We would rather give you that answer and earn the call later than talk you into glass you do not need today.

Not Sure If It Is Repairable?

Tell us where the damage sits on the glass and roughly how big it is in the form above. It costs you nothing to find out, and if it is a repair you will save several hundred dollars by acting now.
